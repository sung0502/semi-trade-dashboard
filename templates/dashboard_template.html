Method and limitations

Source: U.S. Census Bureau International Trade API, HS4 commodity level, monthly values in current U.S. dollars (not inflation-adjusted). Imports use general imports by country of origin; exports use total exports by country of destination.

Country groupings (such as the EU) and regional totals are excluded so nothing is counted twice. Each month, the country rows are reconciled against the Census all-countries total. Recent months are subject to Census revisions.

HHI is the sum of squared percentage shares (0 to 10,000), with bands from the 2023 DOJ/FTC Merger Guidelines. Before/after comparisons show association, not causation. Other factors, such as demand cycles, the 2021 shortage and firm-level investment, move trade at the same time.

Imports of chips by country of origin often reflect where chips were assembled and tested, not where the wafers were made. This is why Malaysia, Vietnam and similar countries can rank above Taiwan.
